Replika is built to feel like an ongoing companion — a relationship-style AI you talk to
regularly. Hope is built around a different goal: helping you reflect on and understand your own
emotional patterns through journaling and mood tracking, not to provide companionship as the core
experience.

If you're looking for an AI you can chat with as a companion, Replika is built for that
specifically. If you're looking for a structured tool to build a reflection habit — journaling,
mood trends, weekly summaries — that's the problem Hope is solving.

Neither tool is a substitute for human relationships or professional mental health support. See
[AI limitations](/ai-limitations) for how Hope is positioned on that point.
