Headspace has built one of the deepest libraries of guided meditation content available, with
structured courses for sleep, focus, stress, and more. That's its core strength, and if a
structured meditation curriculum is specifically what you're looking for, Headspace is built for
exactly that.

Hope's core strength is different: AI-powered journaling and reflection, with mood tracking and
weekly reflection reports as the central habit-building loop. Personalized meditations are part of
Hope's offering, but they're generated from what you've actually been journaling about, rather
than offered as a standalone curriculum to work through.

Some people use both — Headspace for a structured meditation practice, Hope for daily journaling
and mood tracking — since the two tools are solving adjacent but distinct problems.
