ChatGPT is a capable, general-purpose assistant — and plenty of people already use it informally
to talk through their day. Hope isn't trying to out-general ChatGPT; it's a narrower tool, built
specifically around journaling, mood tracking, and reflection, with structure (mood trends,
weekly reports, guided breathing) that a general assistant isn't designed to provide out of the
box.

If what you want is a single flexible assistant for everything, ChatGPT is the better fit. If
what you want is a dedicated space built specifically for a daily reflection habit — with the
structure and history-tracking that habit benefits from — that's what Hope is built for.

Both are AI tools, and both come with the same fundamental limitation: neither is a therapist,
and neither should be relied on for a mental health crisis. See our
[AI limitations](/ai-limitations) page for how Hope handles that distinction.
