Calm has built a large library of relaxation content — sleep stories, soundscapes, and guided
meditations — aimed primarily at winding down and improving sleep. That's a different core job
than what Hope is built for.

Hope's center of gravity is AI-powered journaling: writing through your day, tracking your mood,
and getting a weekly reflection report that turns scattered entries into a pattern you can
actually see. Guided breathing is included, but as a support for in-the-moment stress, not as a
large standalone content library.

If sleep and relaxation audio is the main thing you're after, Calm is built specifically for
that. If a daily reflection and journaling habit is what you're trying to build, that's Hope's
core focus.
